Callaloo

The leafy green at the heart of Caribbean cooking.

Callaloo is a leafy green vegetable found in kitchens across the Caribbean — steamed, sautéed with seasonings, or cooked down as a side dish. In Jamaica it usually refers to amaranth leaves; in Trinidad & Tobago, "callaloo" often means a dish made from dasheen (taro) bush leaves. Both the leaf and the dish are common search terms — FarmFlow lists the fresh leaf.
