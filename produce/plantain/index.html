Plantain

Plantain, green banana and breadfruit — Caribbean tree-crop staples.

Plantain is a Caribbean staple whether it's green and boiled, ripe and fried, or grilled — alongside its close relatives green banana and breadfruit, all commonly listed together on FarmFlow as tree-crop produce.

Tree crops on FarmFlow

  • Plantain — sold green or ripe, by hand (bunch) or individually.
  • Green banana — a boiling staple, distinct from ripe dessert bananas.
  • Breadfruit — roasted, fried, or boiled, sold whole by the piece.

Buying & selling plantain

List your harvest with a photo, price and quantity, or search FarmFlow by island and town for growers near you.

Priced locally: sellers list in JMD, TTD, BBD, EC$, GYD or whichever currency their buyers use.
