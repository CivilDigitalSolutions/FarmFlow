Buy & Sell Produce Online in Jamaica

Jamaica’s farmers, higglers and buyers — connected directly, island-wide.

FarmFlow connects farmers, higglers and buyers right across Jamaica — from ground-provision farms in St. Elizabeth to markets in Kingston, Spanish Town, Montego Bay, Mandeville and beyond. List what you grow, or search for fresh produce near your parish, then message the seller directly to agree a price and arrange collection or delivery.

Popular Jamaican produce on FarmFlow

  • Yellow yam and negro yam — St. Elizabeth, Jamaica's "breadbasket parish", is the island's yam-growing heartland.
  • Ground provisions — cassava, dasheen, coco, and sweet potato.
  • Callaloo, scotch bonnet peppers, pak choi and okra (ochro) — kitchen staples sold fresh, island-wide.
  • Ackee, breadfruit, plantain and june plum — Jamaica's signature tree crops.
  • Pimento (allspice), thyme and escallion — for seasoning and export-style spice blends.

Selling your produce online in Jamaica

Download FarmFlow, create a free listing with a real photo, your price, quantity, and the town where the goods are located — then buyers in your parish and beyond can message you directly on WhatsApp or SMS. Read our guide on how to sell yam online in Jamaica for a step-by-step walkthrough.

Priced in Jamaican dollars: sellers list in JMD (J$), so buyers always know exactly what they're paying.
