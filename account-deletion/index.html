Delete My Account & Data

You can delete your FarmFlow account and the personal data associated with it at any time — from the app, or right here without the app installed.

Option 1 — In the app

Open FarmFlow and go to Account → Settings → Delete my account, then confirm.

Option 2 — On this website

Sign in, open Account and choose Delete my account. Works on any phone or computer, iPhone included — no app needed.

Can't sign in? Email us from the address on your FarmFlow account with the subject "Account deletion request", and we'll verify it's you and delete the account for you.

What happens next

  • Straight away, your account is deactivated: your listings and stall disappear from the marketplace, and you're signed out on every device.
  • Changed your mind? Just sign back in within 14 days. Your account, listings and stall come back exactly as they were.
  • After 14 days, your account is deleted permanently and automatically.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile, including display name, town, contact number and profile photo
  • All of your listings, and their photos and thumbnails
  • Your saved favourites, followers, notifications and preferences
  • Your support messages, verification requests and the records of your activity in the app
  • Your marketing consent record (your consent is withdrawn)

Reports you made about other listings are kept so our moderators can act on them, but your name and account are removed from them.

What we keep, and why

A small amount of data is retained after deletion where the law requires it or where it's needed to protect other users:

DataRetentionReason
Purchase and payment ledger records (such as donations)For as long as accounting and tax law requiresAccounting and tax law
Records of enforcement actions (bans, fraud reports)For as long as needed to protect the platform and its usersPreventing banned users from returning and protecting other users
Verification documents under the Verified Seller Programme (once it launches)For as long as regulatory obligations requireRegulatory record-keeping obligations

Retained records are kept securely, are not used for any other purpose, and are deleted when the retention period ends.

Before you go

  • After the 14 days, deletion is permanent — your listings, favourites, and verified status (if any) cannot be recovered.
  • If you only want to stop marketing emails, you don't need to delete your account: turn off “FarmFlow updates by email” in the app’s Settings or use the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • If something's wrong with the app, tell us — we may be able to fix it.
