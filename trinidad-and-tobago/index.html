Buy & Sell Produce Online in Trinidad & Tobago

From Port of Spain to Chaguanas, San Fernando, Arima and across Tobago.

FarmFlow connects farmers, vendors and buyers across Trinidad & Tobago — alongside the produce economy that NAMDEVCO and local farmers markets already serve. List what you grow, or search for fresh produce near you, from Port of Spain and Chaguanas to San Fernando, Arima, Point Fortin and across Tobago.

Popular Trinidad & Tobago produce on FarmFlow

  • Ground provisions — cassava, dasheen, eddoe, sweet potato and tannia.
  • Callaloo bush, pumpkin, and ochro (okra) — everyday cooking staples.
  • Scotch bonnet and Congo pepper — for pepper sauce and seasoning.
  • Pigeon peas (gungo peas), bodi, and christophene — grown island-wide.
  • Breadfruit and plantain — from backyard trees to farm-gate sales.

Selling your produce online in Trinidad & Tobago

Create a free FarmFlow listing with a photo, price, quantity, and your town — buyers across Trinidad and Tobago can then message you directly to arrange payment and collection. No middleman, no commission on the sale itself.

Priced in Trinidad & Tobago dollars: sellers list in TTD (TT$).
