Buy & Sell Produce Online in Barbados

From Bridgetown to St. James, Christ Church and every parish between.

FarmFlow gives Barbadian farmers and buyers a direct line to each other — no market stall required. List what you grow, or search for fresh produce near you, from Bridgetown and St. Michael to Christ Church, St. James and the country parishes.

Popular Barbados produce on FarmFlow

  • Sweet potato and ground provisions — a Barbadian farming staple.
  • Breadfruit, okra, and cucumber — grown island-wide and sold fresh.
  • Scotch bonnet peppers — for Bajan pepper sauce and seasoning.
  • Callaloo, pumpkin, and christophene — everyday cooking staples.
  • Herbs and seasonings — thyme, marjoram, and fresh chives.

Selling your produce online in Barbados

Create a free FarmFlow listing with a photo, price, quantity and your parish — buyers across Barbados can then message you directly to arrange payment and collection.

Priced in Barbadian dollars: sellers list in BBD (Bds$).
