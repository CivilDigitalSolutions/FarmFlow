How to Sell Yam Online in Jamaica

A step-by-step guide for Jamaican yam farmers and vendors.

Jamaica has an estimated 28,000 yam farmers, most concentrated around St. Elizabeth. If you grow yellow yam, negro yam, or other varieties, FarmFlow gives you a direct, free way to reach buyers across the island — no market stall required.

Step 1 — Download FarmFlow and create an account

Get FarmFlow on Google Play and sign in with email, Google, or Facebook. It's free.

Step 2 — Create your yam listing

  • Tap the + button and choose "Create listing".
  • Add a clear, real photo of your yam — buyers want to see exactly what they're getting.
  • Write an honest title and description — note the variety (yellow yam, negro yam) if you know it.
  • Set your price and quantity in Jamaican dollars (JMD), and confirm the parish or town where the yam is located.
  • Publish. Your listing goes live once it passes moderation.

Step 3 — Respond to buyers

Buyers message you directly through WhatsApp or SMS to ask questions, agree a price, and arrange collection or delivery. FarmFlow doesn't process payment — that happens directly between you and the buyer.

Step 4 — Meet and trade safely

Meet in daylight, in a public place if possible, and inspect quantity before money changes hands. Read our full Safety Tips before your first trade.

Step 5 — Keep your listing current

Mark your listing as sold once your yam is gone, and relist when you have a fresh harvest. Accurate, up-to-date listings build buyer trust and rank better in search.
