Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the rights you have over your data.

Effective date: 21 October 2026 · Version 1.4 · Applies to the FarmFlow Android app and this website, including the FarmFlow web marketplace

1. Who we are

FarmFlow is an agricultural marketplace app for the Caribbean, operated by Civil Digital ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for personal data processed through FarmFlow. You can reach us at info@civildigital.co.uk.

3. Photos and images

What we store. When you add a profile photo or a photo to a listing, the image is stored with our cloud storage provider on our behalf. We store only a processed version of your photo — not the original file from your device.

Photos are compressed and a preview is generated automatically. Before upload, your photo is resized and re-encoded on your own device so a smaller file is sent than your camera produced. We also generate a small preview image, used in search results, listing cards and seller pages; the full-size photo is only downloaded when you open a listing itself. No separate action is needed from you for this.

Unnecessary image metadata is removed. Photo files often carry hidden information such as the date and time, device details, and — where recorded — the GPS coordinates of where a picture was taken. Because we re-encode your photo, none of that information is carried into the file we store; only the picture itself, correctly oriented, is kept.

Why we process images this way. Compression and thumbnails make FarmFlow faster to use and reduce the mobile data and storage the app uses. Removing metadata means hidden details in your photos are not published alongside your listing.

Deletion. Deleting one of your listings permanently deletes its photo and thumbnail straight away. If a listing expires and is not renewed, we keep its images for up to 90 days so you can renew the listing, after which they are permanently deleted, along with any other images no longer attached to a listing or account. Uploading a new profile photo permanently replaces the previous one — we do not keep a history of past photos.

Photos from social sign-in. If you sign in with a third-party account and we show your profile picture from that account, that image is served by the provider and is not copied into our storage. It is governed by that provider's own privacy policy. Removing it is done through your account with that provider, or by uploading your own photo to FarmFlow instead.

4. Your listings and stall on the public web

FarmFlow is a public marketplace. Listings and seller stall pages are published on this website, where anyone can view them without an account, and they may be indexed by search engines such as Google and shown in search results.

  • Listing pages show the produce title, description and photos, price, quantity, category, the town and island it is in, and the seller's verification badge. They do not name the seller to visitors who are not signed in.
  • Seller stall pages (the page your stock sheet's QR code opens) show your display name, profile photo, “about” text, town and island, follower count, time on FarmFlow, verification badge, and your active listings.
  • Never published on the web: your phone number, your email address, and your precise location. A seller's contact number is shown only to signed-in FarmFlow users, as in the app.

When a listing is sold, expires or is deleted, or a seller account is suspended or removed, its page stops being available on our website within a few minutes. Search engines and other sites may keep copies for a while after that; we ask search engines to drop removed pages, but we do not control their copies. If you do not want a listing to appear publicly, do not publish it.

5. Advertising

FarmFlow shows advertising provided by Google, in the Android app (Google AdMob) and on this website (Google AdSense). Ads appear among search results and on listing pages, and are always labelled “Sponsored”.

In the app, Google may process your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

On the website, Google uses cookies and similar technologies, and receives information such as your IP address and the page you are viewing, to serve and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud. If you have consented, it may also personalise ads based on your activity on other sites. Google acts under its own privacy policy; you can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and turn off personalised ads at Google's ad settings. Ads are not loaded at all on pages where we don't show them, such as signing in, your account and donating.

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area (EEA), we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads, using a consent management prompt — in the app, and on the website the first time an ad would appear. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: in the app from Settings → Manage ad consent, and on the website from the Privacy & cookie settings link at the foot of every page.

6. Marketing emails

We only send marketing emails to people who have opted in. Marketing is never on by default — you choose to receive it. If you opt in, you can withdraw your consent and unsubscribe at any time, either from the link in any marketing email or by emailing info@civildigital.co.uk. Transactional messages that are part of the service (for example, a listing being approved) are not marketing and are always sent while your account is active.

7. What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not track your precise or continuous location.
  • FarmFlow has no in-app chat. When you use a contact button, your conversation happens in your messaging or SMS app, between you and the other person — FarmFlow does not see or store it.
  • We do not send marketing emails without your opt-in consent.

8. Payments

Buyer–seller transactions take place directly between users, outside the app; we do not process or hold those payments. Optional donations are processed through our app store's billing system in the Android app, and on the website by our payment processor, Stripe. When you donate on the website you enter your payment details on Stripe's own secure checkout page; Stripe processes them under its own privacy policy and sends you a receipt, and we receive only the donation record described in section 2. Donating does not require a FarmFlow account and gives no benefits in the app. If we introduce paid services in future, such as boosted listings or Verified Seller Programme fees, they will be processed by a third-party payment processor under its own privacy policy.

9. Who we share data with

  • Service providers — our trusted cloud hosting and infrastructure providers (covering account sign-in, backend and website hosting, analytics, push notifications, in-app purchases, and advertising), our payment processors (including Stripe for website donations), and our email delivery provider, each processing data only on our instructions. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your device's IP address when a page loads.
  • Other users and the public — your display name, profile town, and listings are visible to other users, and if you sell, so is the contact phone number you add for buyers (to signed-in users only), and listings and seller stall pages are also public on the web (see section 4); your email address is not.
  • Messaging apps — FarmFlow includes contact buttons that open a messaging or SMS app on your device to connect buyers and sellers directly. Using one passes the relevant contact number to that app; anything you then send is handled under that app's own privacy policy or terms, not this one.
  • Authorities — where required by law or to protect users from serious harm.

10. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, personal data is deleted or anonymised promptly, except records we must keep longer for legal, security, or accounting reasons (for example, purchase and donation ledgers and consent records). Details of what is removed are on our Account & Data Deletion page. Photos and images have their own retention rules, set out in section 3 above.

11. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2020 (Jamaica) and the Data Protection Act, Chap. 22:04 (Trinidad & Tobago), you have rights to access, correct, and delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent (such as marketing consent) at any time. To exercise any right, email info@civildigital.co.uk. You may also complain to your local data protection authority: the Office of the Information Commissioner (Jamaica) or the Office of the Information Commissioner (Trinidad & Tobago).

12. Children

FarmFlow is designed for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18, though anyone may browse listings. We do not knowingly collect personal data from, or identify, anyone under 18 — you must be 18 or over to create an account. If we learn that we hold data about someone under 18, we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll publish the new version on this page before it takes effect — at least 14 days in advance for material changes. Each version is dated and numbered at the top of this page.
