Buy & Sell Produce Online in Guyana

From Georgetown to the Essequibo Coast, Berbice and the Demerara region.

Guyana's large-scale agriculture and small farm-gate trade both have a home on FarmFlow. List what you grow, or search for fresh produce near you, from Georgetown and the Demerara region to the Essequibo Coast and Berbice.

Popular Guyanese produce on FarmFlow

  • Cassava, eddoe and ground provisions — grown across the coastal belt and interior.
  • Plantain and pumpkin — everyday staples sold fresh.
  • Callaloo, bora (long bean), and boulanger (eggplant) — kitchen-garden crops sold locally.
  • Wiri wiri and scotch bonnet peppers — for pepper sauce and seasoning.
  • Rice and other farm provisions — from smallholders alongside Guyana's larger rice economy.

Selling your produce online in Guyana

Create a free FarmFlow listing with a photo, price, quantity and your region — buyers across Guyana can then message you directly to arrange payment and collection.

Priced in Guyanese dollars: sellers list in GYD (G$).
