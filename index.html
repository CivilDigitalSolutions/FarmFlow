How it works

No payments in the app — FarmFlow just makes the introduction.

1

Browse or list

Search produce by category, island and town — or create a free listing for what you're selling in minutes.

2

Message directly

Contact the seller on WhatsApp or SMS to agree a price, quantity, and where to meet. FarmFlow never processes payments.

3

Meet & trade

Collect or arrange delivery, pay the seller directly in person, and leave feedback. Read our Safety Tips first.
