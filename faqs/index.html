Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we're asked most.

Getting started

Is FarmFlow free to use?

Yes. Creating an account, browsing, messaging, and listing items are all free. We may add optional paid extras in future, such as boosted listings, but everyday buying and selling will stay free.

Which islands does FarmFlow cover?

FarmFlow serves farmers, vendors and buyers across the Caribbean, including Jamaica, Trinidad & Tobago, Barbados and Guyana. Listings are organised by island and town, so search can sort listings nearest first.

What kind of produce can I find on FarmFlow?

Everything from ground provisions — yam, cassava, dasheen, sweet potato — to callaloo, scotch bonnet peppers, plantain, breadfruit, herbs, homemade preserves and more. Browse the full list on our produce page.

What currency are prices listed in?

Sellers set their own prices in their local currency — Jamaican dollars (JMD), Trinidad & Tobago dollars (TTD), Barbadian dollars (BBD), Eastern Caribbean dollars (EC$), Guyanese dollars (GYD), and others across the region. Currency is shown on the listing itself.

Can I browse without an account?

Yes — choose "Continue as guest" on the welcome screen to browse listings. You'll need an account to message sellers, save favourites, or list items of your own.

Selling

How do I become a seller?

Any registered user can sell. From the app:

  • Tap Sell your produce to switch on selling, then ＋ Create Listing on your Dashboard (or + New on My Listings).
  • Add photos, a title, price, quantity, and the listing's town.
  • Tap Publish Listing. It goes live straight away — listings that break the rules can be removed by our moderators.

The FarmFlow Android app, showing sample listings.

Before your first listing, read the Prohibited Items Policy — some categories are restricted.

How do I become a Verified Seller?

In the app today, a Verified badge is free: open your profile, tap Get verified and tell us about your farm, and a moderator reviews it. The full Verified Seller Programme, which will open restricted categories such as agricultural machinery, livestock and meat, is coming later. It will involve identity verification plus category-specific documents (for example, dealer registration with your national agricultural authority, or livestock traceability enrolment), and fees per category.

Full requirements and how to apply are on the Verified Seller Programme page.

What is a boosted listing?

Boosts are a possible future option that would give a listing extra visibility for a set period. They aren't available yet — if we introduce them, any fees will be set out before you buy.

Why was my listing removed?

Listings are removed when they breach our Community Guidelines or Prohibited Items Policy — most commonly items that can't be listed yet (such as livestock, meat or machinery), misleading photos, or duplicate posts. You'll receive a notification explaining why, and you can appeal by tapping Contact support on the notice in your Seller Dashboard, or by contacting support.

Payments & safety

Does FarmFlow handle payments between buyers and sellers?

No. FarmFlow connects buyers and sellers; the transaction itself happens between you, usually in person. We never ask you to pay for goods through the app, and you should treat any request to do so as a scam. See our Safety Tips.

How do I report a suspicious listing or user?

Tap the flag icon on any listing or seller profile to report it. Reports are reviewed by human moderators. For urgent issues, contact support directly.

Your account & data

How do I stop marketing emails?

Marketing emails are only sent if you opted in. Turn them off any time in the app under Account → Settings → “FarmFlow updates by email”, or use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any marketing email. Service emails (like security notices) are separate and always sent.

How do I delete my account?

From the app: Account → Settings → Request data deletion, which emails us your request. Or from this website — no app needed: Delete My Account & Data. Deletion is permanent.

Why does FarmFlow ask for my location?

We use your device's approximate location only when you tap Use my location — to suggest your nearest town, or to place a listing for distance sorting. We never track you. You can skip this and pick your town manually. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

What happens to the photos I upload?

Your photo is resized and re-encoded on your own phone before it is uploaded, so a smaller image than your camera produced is what leaves your device. Re-encoding also strips the hidden details photo files usually carry — the date and time, your phone model, and any GPS coordinates of where the picture was taken. Only the picture itself is stored.

We create a small preview of every listing photo automatically, so browsing uses less of your mobile data; the full image downloads only when someone opens the listing.

Delete a listing and its photo goes with it straight away. If a listing expires and you don't renew it, we keep the photo for 90 days so you can bring the listing back, then delete it. Uploading a new profile photo replaces the old one, which is deleted — we don't keep a history. Full details are in section 3 of our Privacy Policy.
